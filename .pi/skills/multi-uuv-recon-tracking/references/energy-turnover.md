# Energy and Entity Generations

Energy percentage and remaining range share one backend model. Exit is required before the remaining energy falls below feasible exit cost plus reserve. Never wait for zero energy.

An approved standing policy may authorize bounded energy exit, boundary replacement and local coverage repair. It does not authorize arbitrary mission changes. Otherwise request approval through normal plan submission.

A departing entity follows a safe forward route across the external boundary. The runtime atomically replaces it with a fully charged inward-facing entity at a safe entry. Exactly eight slots remain visible. A blocked exit/entry produces an explicit protection pause, never an internal teleport.

Identity is `(slot_id, generation)`. Old commands, observations, approvals and trajectories must not be attributed to a replacement generation. Coverage history and contact estimates persist. A replacement is idle until an authorized feasible assignment exists.
