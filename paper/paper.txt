          基于主动学习深度神经网络模型的结构可靠性分析
                    方法
                                                                翟东升1 雷宗建2 罗辉1,3
                                          (1．石河子大学水利建筑工程学院，新疆 石河子 832003；
                                           2．湖北交投荆楚建设管理有限公司，湖北 武汉 430072；
                                          3．华中科技大学土木与水利工程学院，湖北 武汉 430072)

               摘要 为提高蒙特卡罗结构可靠性分析中极限状态面附近关键样本的获取能力，提出一种基于主动学习深度神经
               网络的结构可靠性分析方法。该方法包括初始建模和主动学习更新两个阶段。首先，在随机变量空间中构建初始
               实验设计，并建立初始深度神经网络代理模型。随后，根据代理模型预测结果筛选极限状态面附近的候选样本。
               结合样本分布特征选取新增样本，并根据其真实函数值调整实验设计。在此基础上，迭代更新代理模型，直至失
               效概率估计满足收敛要求。通过二维多失效模式函数、屋面桁架、40 维函数和桁架桥有限元模型验证所提方法。
               结果表明，该方法在多失效模式、高维及隐式可靠性问题中均具有较好的失效概率估计精度和效率。

              Reliability analysis method based on an active learning deep neural network
                                                 model
                                                       Dongsheng Zhai1              Zongjian Lei ²   Hui Luo1,3
                 (1．College of Water Conservancy & Architectural Engineering, Shihezi University, Shihezi 832003, China ；
                              2．Hubei Jiaotou Jingchu Construction Management Co., Ltd., Wuhan 430072, Hubei, China;
           3．School of Civil and Hydraulic Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan 430072, China)


         Abstract    To improve the acquisition of critical samples near the limit state surface in Monte Carlo simulation-based structural
         reliability analysis, an active learning deep neural network method is proposed. The method consists of two stages: initial modeling and
         active learning updating. First, an initial design of experiments is constructed in the random variable space, and an initial deep neural
         network surrogate model is established. Subsequently, candidate samples near the limit state surface are identified based on the surrogate
         model predictions. New samples are then selected by considering their spatial distribution, and the design of experiments is adjusted
         according to their true limit state function values. The surrogate model is iteratively updated until the failure probability estimate satisfies
         the convergence criterion. The proposed method was validated using a two-dimensional multi-failure-mode function, a roof truss, a 40-
         dimensional function, and a finite element model of a truss bridge. The results showed that the proposed method achieved good failure
         probability estimation accuracy and computational efficiency for multi-failure-mode, high-dimensional, and implicit reliability
         problems.
         Key words     reliability analysis; surrogate model; deep neural networks; active learning; limit state surface
